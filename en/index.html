Mahjongverse

One board. Fourteen worlds.

Match free tiles, clear layered boards and follow the portals. Every ten levels, the journey changes its scenery, symbols and soundtrack.

  • 14 universes

    From a jade garden to the Sands of the Nile, from Neo-Tokyo to Astral Cosmos. Every world has its own art, tiles and music.

  • 182 fusions

    Clear level 140 to witness the fracture. Then one world brings the scenery and another brings the tile symbols.

  • 1000+ levels

    Every board starts with a solution. The challenge grows as you progress, with a boss every ten levels.

  • At your own pace

    Hint, undo and shuffle when you need them. Portrait or landscape, six languages and a Reduce motion setting.

Who makes it

MAMCJ Games is run by one person, in Brazil, with the Godot engine. The games are built for a pocket and a short break: they start fast, save on their own and never ask you to create an account.
