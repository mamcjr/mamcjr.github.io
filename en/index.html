Mahjongverse

One board. Fourteen worlds.

Match free tiles, clear layered boards and follow the portals. Every ten levels, the journey changes its scenery, symbols and soundtrack.

  • 14 universes

    From a jade garden to the Sands of the Nile, from Neo-Tokyo to Astral Cosmos. Every world has its own art, tiles and music.

  • 182 fusions

    Clear level 140 to witness the fracture. Then one world brings the scenery and another brings the tile symbols.

  • 1000+ levels

    Every board starts with a solution. The challenge grows as you progress, with a boss every ten levels.

  • At your own pace

    Hint, undo and shuffle when you need them. Portrait or landscape, six languages and a Reduce motion setting.

Dawn Gambit · Folklore Chess

Legends take the board.

The Night Haunts have stolen dawn. Guide the Forest Guardians across six Brazilian biomes in a journey of 3D chess.

    A six-chapter campaign with illustrated scenes and progress saved on your device.

  • Arcade

  • Twelve legendary beings

    Brazilian folklore characters, animated captures and an illustrated book about the legends.

  • Your way

    Portrait or landscape, English or Portuguese, and graphics, audio and animation settings.

The studio

Made in Brazil.
With imagination.

MAMCJ Games is an independent studio run by one person in Brazil, with the Godot engine. Each game is a new chance to build a world worth discovering.

Built for your pocket and a short break: the games start fast, save on their own and never ask you to create an account.
