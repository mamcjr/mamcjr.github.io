The game

Mahjongverse

Every ten levels you step through a portal into a new universe, with its own colors, animated background, music and even tile symbols. After level 140 the universes begin to fuse.

  • 14 universes

    Jade Dynasty, Sands of the Nile, Runic Fjord, Olympus, Astral Cosmos, Mayan Jungle, Neo-Tokyo, Alchemy Lab, Atlantis, Celtic Grove, Chrome City, 8-Bit World, Noir City and Jazz Age.

  • 1000+ solvable levels

    Every board is generated with a guaranteed solution. Difficulty rises every three levels, and every tenth level is a boss.

  • Made to unwind

    An original soundtrack for every universe, hint, undo and shuffle when you need them, and up to three stars per level.

  • Six languages

    English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian, with achievements and cloud saves through Google Play Games.

Who makes it

MAMCJ Games is run by one person, in Brazil, with the Godot engine. The games are built for a pocket and a short break: they start fast, save on their own and never ask you to create an account.
