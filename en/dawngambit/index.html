Journey of Dawn

Night has taken Brazil.
Your next move matters.

The Night Haunts have stolen dawn. Curupira gathers the Forest Guardians to challenge Cuca's spell and bring morning back to the Brazilian biomes.

Travel through six chapters, follow illustrated scenes and bring chess strategy to the stone board. The rules are classic; the characters are our legends.

  • A journey to return to

    A six-chapter story with progress saved on your device so you can continue later.

  • A match at your pace

    In Arcade, choose five computer difficulty levels or two players on the same device.

  • A living chessboard

    3D characters and animated captures, with animation speed, audio and graphics settings.

Before you play

One move at a time.

Can I download it now?

The internal test is in preparation. There is no public download yet. Write to contato@mamcjgames.com to learn more; sending an email does not guarantee test access.

Can I play with another person?

Yes, Arcade supports two players on the same device. This version does not include online multiplayer.

Do I need an account?

The game does not have its own account registration or login. Campaign progress, matches and preferences are saved locally on your device.

Does the game have ads?

There are rewarded ads for assistance features such as hints and undo. They require internet access and are configured as test ads in this version. Read the privacy policy for information on data handling.

Which devices and languages?

This version was prepared for Android 10 or later, on 64-bit arm64 devices. Compatibility and performance must be confirmed in Google Play and on each device. English and Portuguese are available, with portrait and landscape layouts.
