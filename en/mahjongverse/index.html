Before the next match

How do I play?

Match two identical free tiles: a tile must have nothing covering it and at least one free side. Complete the pairs to clear the board.

What changes between worlds?

Illustrated scenery, symbols, tile materials, particles and soundtrack. After level 140, the universes begin to fuse.

Can I reduce the animations?

Yes. Turn on Reduce motion in Settings. The preference is saved, and transitions keep the story messages.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads, purchases and Google Play Games services need a connection.
